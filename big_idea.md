# New Section (to insert after the Hero)

# 29. The Big Idea

## Purpose

Immediately after capturing attention in the Hero, this section answers the visitor's subconscious question:

> "What is this book really about?"

Rather than introducing features or chapters, this section presents the core philosophy that underpins the entire book.

It should be concise, memorable, and emotionally resonant—functioning as the website's thesis statement.

---

## Design Goal

This section should provide a brief pause between the visually rich Hero and the more informational sections that follow.

It should feel calm, spacious, and reflective.

---

## Height

Approximately 60–70% of the viewport height on desktop.

On mobile, the content should fit comfortably within one screen without excessive scrolling.

---

## Background

Solid white with generous whitespace.

A faint gold radial glow behind the central message may be used to subtly reinforce the theme of inspiration.

---

## Layout

Single-column, centered.

```text
Small Label

↓

Large Statement

↓

Supporting Paragraph
```

---

## Eyebrow Label

Small uppercase text.

Suggested:

**THE BIG IDEA**

Color:

Deep Red (#B10E16)

---

## Primary Statement

Large typography.

Maximum two lines.

Suggested copy:

> Every invention, every movement, and every positive change in history began as an idea in someone's mind.

---

## Supporting Paragraph

Maximum 80 words.

Example:

> This book encourages children to dream boldly, ask thoughtful questions, embrace technology with purpose, and discover that even the biggest changes in the world often begin with one curious mind.

Maximum content width:

700px.

---

## Decorative Elements

Extremely restrained.

Possible elements:

- one faint light bulb outline
- a subtle constellation of dots
- a thin holographic line
- a tiny globe motif

Opacity should remain below 8%.

The emphasis should remain on the message rather than the decoration.

---

## Animation

As the section enters the viewport:

- Eyebrow label fades in.
- Main statement rises gently into place.
- Supporting paragraph fades in after a brief delay.

Total animation duration:

Approximately 600ms.

Animations should occur only once.

---

## Success Criteria

Visitors should leave this section with a clear understanding of the book's central message before continuing further down the page.
